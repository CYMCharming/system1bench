\section{What historical controls establish}
\label{sec:historical}
The historical collection is an atlas of decision functions, not a pooled test set. In four candidate-order controls, changing the presentation produces heterogeneous prediction turnover even when the reference labels and underlying state are fixed. Banking77 is particularly diagnostic: Laya English changes 48\% of selected intents under reversal while gaining nine percentage points in reference accuracy; Laya Multilingual changes 38\% with \emph{zero} net accuracy change. Llama changes 65\% under reversal versus 4\% under same-order repeat and loses 25 points. The exact transition counts and cluster intervals are in Appendix~\ref{app:historical-controls}. Neither unchanged accuracy nor a small error-rate difference is evidence of per-case stability. Since reversal may also reassign answer tokens for the LLM adapters, we call it a \emph{joint interface} intervention until the orthogonal codebook test in Section~\ref{sec:domain-codebook}.

Historical labels vary in authority. An annotation measures agreement with its dataset's task convention; an authored or synthetic teacher label does not establish independent ground truth. To test a stronger reference type, we froze 288 executable-policy base states, 96 each for refund, access and routing, before running the specified variants. Jev is correct on 100\% of original and jointly perturbed refund and access actions, but on only 54.2\% of original and 29.2\% of jointly perturbed routing actions. Thus low turnover can coexist with failure to apply the supplied rule. The fresh diagnostic also includes one-field counterfactuals, distractors, wording, language and output-encoding changes. We report the full six-effect family and its within-family simultaneous intervals in the supplement, not a selected favorable intervention as an overall benchmark score.

One post-hoc error inspection suggested that Jev confused a routing conjunction with disjunction, but a separately generated held-out wording test did not yield a universal fix: explicit wording improved 7/48 to 13/48 English exclusive-or cases while reducing the analogous Chinese cell from 46/48 to 13/48. This negative check is useful because it prevents turning an error pattern into an unsupported repair mechanism. It motivates a domain extension whose references originate outside our authored policies.
