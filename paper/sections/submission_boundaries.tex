\section{Boundaries and conclusion}
The same benchmark score can move for reasons outside the intended decision function. On a frozen 212-state, 15-source challenge, brief zero-shot deliberation changes 55 Llama and 46 Qwen decisions relative to one-pass code logits. Llama gains 12 net correct cases (25 corrections, 13 regressions; three invalid generations), but its source-equal effect is $+4.7$ points with interval $[-1.4,+10.6]$. Qwen loses five net correct cases (12 corrections, 17 regressions; ten invalid). A post-hoc parser would recover eight of those Qwen invalid answers; strict output compliance is the registered result. This is evidence that output grammar and prompt are part of the evaluated adapter, not that one short deliberation prompt bounds the model's ability (Appendix~\ref{sec:generative}).

An independent natural-domain control isolates a simpler adapter concern. On the identical 144 legal and 180 three-class science pairs, greedy direct generation with the \emph{unchanged} historical code prompt and strict whole-response one-code parsing reproduces every constrained next-token code-logit label for both 8B models: 648/648 valid outputs and zero paired semantic flips. This rules out a direct-code parsing discrepancy on these samples, not a best-prompt advantage or probability equivalence.

\paragraph{Source-validity audit.} ContractNLI repeats 17 fixed hypotheses; 43/48 sampled contradictions use just four hypothesis IDs. A document-blind lookup trained on the official training contracts' modal label per hypothesis reaches 82/144 (56.9\%) on our balanced sample. This exposes a label-prior shortcut, not a fair zero-shot baseline or evidence that our systems use it. The 16,000-character cap excludes 24/123 legal test contracts, including 13/23 SEC-HTML contracts, so the selected format mix is not neutral. SciFact's two-label diagnostic excludes no-information and retrieval. In the three-way extension, \textsc{NoInfo} means an official cited pair without annotated abstract evidence; absence of annotation is a weaker reference than an explicit evidence relation. The reported intervals are conditional on selected or eligible cited pairs from this source and pinned adapters. Even the full cited-pair census is not a new population sample; it does not establish a prevalence-weighted legal or general scientific score, a deployed-system outcome, or causality at the neural-mechanism level. Native-head and LLM code probabilities have different semantics and should not be pooled as calibrated confidence.

The actionable contribution is consequently a measurement protocol: attach reference provenance to each source, preserve paired case identity, separate same-request variation from specified interface changes, report prediction discordance and both-correct coverage alongside accuracy, and isolate code assignment when using short-token adapters. The independent domains and negative controls show why these coordinates are needed; they do not yield a universal robustness winner. Reproducible request contracts, source/licensing notes and remaining validity audits are described in Appendix~\ref{app:domain-validity} and the companion artifact.
