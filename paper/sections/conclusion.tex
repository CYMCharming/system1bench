\section{Conclusion}
System1Bench separates reference agreement, correctness turnover, prediction stability and operational efficiency at a fixed typed decision interface. The cross-domain atlas makes workload coverage and reference provenance explicit without treating unlike datasets as one score. Matched controls show that substantial corrections and regressions can cancel in aggregate accuracy, while the held-out wording test rejects a simple universal explanation of one striking routing result. Question-specific context and workload-specific performance provide further evidence against premature pooling. These are measurements of downstream decision behavior, not whole-system task completion; deployment claims require validation of the complete workflow.
