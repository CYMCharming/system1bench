# New-seed policy replication — registered after discovery, before inference

The expansion-v1 four-native-model read suggests nonuniform Kev-4B→Kev-9B
changes, especially a lower routing counterfactual joint-success rate. This
study was designed AFTER that observation; it is a prospective replication,
not a retroactive preregistration of the discovery. Publish positive, null and
contrary results for ALL THREE policy families, not routing alone.

Generate 96 new base states per family using seeds 404, 505 and 606 and the
unchanged canonical draw/priority rules, with original/repeat/reversal/single-
fact counterfactual variants and all three questions retained together.
Exclude any original OR counterfactual fact/parameter state matching either
state of any expansion-v1 policy pair; prohibit duplicate original/CF states
within this new replication. This is 288 independent new pairs, 1,152 requests
and 3,456 decisions per model. Check every label against BOTH executable
oracles and verify that exactly one fact changes and the correct action changes.
The familiar rule templates and finite parameter space are NOT a new domain,
human construct validation, unseen training distribution, or new rule grammar.

Evaluate exactly the expansion-v1 pinned Kev-4B and Kev-9B with the SAME
complete, merged BF16/SDPA official native adapter, stored temperatures,
single-request inference, question packing, and no augmentation/ensembling.
No test-fitting calibration. Record input, runner, adapter, model and protocol
hashes. No outputs from other experiments are substituted for new inference.

Six primary paired differences (9B minus 4B): base ACTION correctness and
jointly correct original/counterfactual ACTION pairs for each of refund,
access and routing. Give exact numerator/denominator plus pointwise 95% and
Bonferroni 99.1667% paired state-cluster bootstrap intervals (20,000 resamples,
seed 20261001). The latter is a conservative six-effect family guard, not a
claim of an across-domain architecture/size law. Model training histories
remain confounded with size. Repeat/reversal, primitive-specific results and
cross-head completeness are supplementary. Do not suppress counterexamples.
