# Attribution and data-use boundaries for the natural-domain extension

The paper's legal/scientific extension draws on third-party data. The System1Bench code license does **not** grant rights over upstream contracts, claims, annotations, titles or abstracts. This file is attribution and provenance documentation, not legal advice. Consult the upstream licenses and terms before redistributing a reconstructed freeze.

| Content in the local reconstructed `frozen.json` | Upstream attribution and license notice | Changes here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ContractNLI NDA text, fixed hypotheses and test annotations | Hitachi America, Ltd., **ContractNLI: A Dataset for Document-level Natural Language Inference for Contracts**, via the [official project](https://stanfordnlp.github.io/contract-nli/) and [dataset repository](https://github.com/stanfordnlp/contract-nli). The downloaded archive's `TERMS` states availability under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Cite the [ContractNLI paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01799). | Only test contracts at most 16,000 characters are eligible; 144 document--hypothesis pairs are selected with a fixed hash and balanced across three labels. Complete original selected text and hypothesis are passed as state. |
| SciFact claim text and evidence relation annotations | Allen Institute for AI, **SciFact**, via the [official repository](https://github.com/allenai/scifact), [data documentation](https://github.com/allenai/scifact/blob/master/doc/data.md) and [license notice](https://github.com/allenai/scifact/blob/master/LICENSE.md). The upstream notice assigns CC BY 4.0 to claims/annotations; cite the [SciFact paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14974). | Only 120 positively annotated claim--cited-paper pairs from the labeled development set are selected, 60 per SUPPORT/CONTRADICT class. No unannotated pair is relabeled NOINFO. |
| Scientific paper titles and abstracts in SciFact's corpus | The SciFact [license notice](https://github.com/allenai/scifact/blob/master/LICENSE.md) points to S2ORC abstract-corpus terms under [ODC-By 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). Database attribution does not automatically settle independent rights in every underlying work. | The selected abstract sentences are joined with newline separators; there is no summarization or evidence-span injection. |

The official archive SHA-256 values and the exact source-file SHA-256 values are in `manifest.json`. `prepare.py` rebuilds the local frozen requests from the official archives and refuses to overwrite a previous freeze. `frozen.json` is deliberately excluded from Git because it contains substantial upstream text; benchmark results and figures contain IDs, labels, derived metrics and output probabilities but not the source contract or abstract text. The frozen-file SHA-256 is recorded in `manifest.json` so a reviewer can verify a locally reconstructed copy. Accessing ContractNLI entails accepting its upstream terms. Neither upstream source endorses System1Bench.

If publishing a distributable copy of `frozen.json`, preserve this notice, the upstream notices and the source-paper citations, disclose the selection and newline-joining changes above, and confirm any further rights needed for the particular distribution. Avoid labeling this extension as complete legal advice, contract review, scientific retrieval or comprehensive fact-checking.
