# SciFact cited-pair census: attribution and redistribution boundary

This analysis is derived from **SciFact** by David Wadden, Shanchuan Lin, Kyle Lo, Lucy Lu Wang, Madeleine van Zuylen, Arman Cohan, and Hannaneh Hajishirzi. Cite [*Fact or Fiction: Verifying Scientific Claims*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14974) and the [official dataset repository](https://github.com/allenai/scifact). The [upstream license notice](https://github.com/allenai/scifact/blob/master/LICENSE.md) assigns **CC BY 4.0** to claims and evidence annotations in `claims_*.jsonl`, **ODC-By 1.0** to the S2ORC abstract corpus (`corpus.jsonl`), and Apache 2.0 to upstream code. Database attribution does not necessarily resolve separate rights in every underlying paper abstract. This is provenance information, not legal advice.

The locally frozen `frozen.json` contains original claims, titles, and complete abstract sentences for all **339 distinct, eligible cited claim–document pairs** in official SciFact dev. It is Git-ignored rather than redistributed through this repository. Relative to upstream files, the preparer de-duplicates one repeated citation ID, pairs each cited document with its abstract, joins abstract sentences with newlines, derives `NOINFO` only for an explicitly cited pair without an evidence entry, and creates three paired variants per base request: byte-identical repeat, reversed criterion order, and title-field removal. The title-removed question still mentions a paper title; this instruction/state mismatch is disclosed in `PROTOCOL.md`. The upstream archive and exact source SHA-256 hashes, plus the reconstructed freeze hash, are in `manifest.json`.

If distributing reconstructed requests or abstracts separately, retain source attribution and upstream notices, describe those transformations, and independently confirm any additional rights required. Neither SciFact's creators nor S2ORC endorse System1Bench.
