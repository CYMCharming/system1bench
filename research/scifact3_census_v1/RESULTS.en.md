# SciFact three-choice cited-pair census: post-hoc replication

This is a **same-source, post-hoc full-census replication** of the previously frozen 180-pair, class-balanced three-choice SciFact diagnostic. The protocol was written before the fresh model runs (`PROTOCOL.md`, SHA-256 `927b60f0708b8b268da7ba98ebb8c08f1c994f6271937ed4dfc88999318f9c88`). It does not constitute an independent domain, dataset, or external holdout. The 339 eligible official-dev claim–cited-document pairs consist of **138 SUPPORT, 71 CONTRADICT, and 130 NOINFO** over 300 claims and 283 documents. The **159-pair complement** to the earlier balanced three-choice selection consists of 78/11/70 by those labels, over 141 claims and 144 documents. Thirty-seven positive complement pairs (32 SUPPORT, 5 CONTRADICT) appeared in the still earlier *two-choice* SciFact experiment; thus “previously unevaluated” below means only unevaluated under the three-choice conditions. The census was rerun fresh in full, not assembled from old predictions.

`NOINFO` is derived only for an *explicitly cited* document without an abstract-evidence entry in the official annotations. It is not an independently adjudicated negative relation, a conclusion about a full paper, or a negative label for arbitrary unannotated corpus documents. The tested system receives the cited abstract; there is no retrieval or evidence-span search. The reversal is a **joint interface intervention**: criterion order changes and code-likelihood adapters may change candidate-code assignment. The separate 2×2 factorial experiment may identify interaction; this census alone does not isolate either component. The `title_removed` condition deletes only `state.paper_title`, leaving an instruction that still mentions title; it is a visibility stressor with an instruction/state mismatch, not a clean causal shortcut ablation.

## Full-census and complement outcomes

Each system received all **1,356** frozen requests (339 pairs × base, byte-identical repeat, joint option reversal, title removal). Table counts are reference-correct strict counts, with an invalid decision in the fixed denominator. `Flip` is a semantic base→reversal change among valid pairs; `Rep` is the same for the exact repeat. The complement is a subset of the full census, **not an independent additional sample**.

| Laya Multilingual | 150→150 | 77→80 | 8→8 | 63 / 0 | 0 |

The Jev invalid is `InvalidDecision` on `scifact3-dev:213:13625993` under `title_removed`; it is retained, not silently repaired or dropped. All base and reversal outputs across five systems are valid. Jev client metadata reports full HTTP payloads but cannot establish server-side tokenizer/context behavior. Four local adapter input audits found no truncation among 1,356 requests each. The exact-repeat control is zero flips for all four local systems and four flips for hosted Jev in the full census (two in the complement).

Qwen's earlier cancellation pattern survives the natural cited-pair mix and the complement. In all 339, its total strict accuracy changes **231→220/339** (−3.2 percentage points; paired **claim-cluster** 10,000-draw percentile 95% CI [−8.4,+2.0] pp; document-cluster sensitivity [−8.6,+2.1]), while its NOINFO slice changes **112→68/130** (−33.8 pp; claim [−42.1,−26.0], document [−42.5,−25.6]). SUPPORT rises 110→127/138 (+12.3 pp, claim [6.7,18.4]) and CONTRADICT 9→25/71 (+22.5 pp, claim [13.0,31.9]), offsetting the NOINFO losses. Qwen's *prediction marginal* shifts from base S/C/N = 144/11/184 to reverse 216/33/90; among all 339, 95 base-NOINFO predictions leave that class and one non-NOINFO prediction enters it. This is a systematic redistribution, not a one-label score typo or merely an aggregate leaderboard movement.

Most importantly, the complement-only check retains the pattern: total **129→119/159** (−6.3 pp; claim [−13.7,+1.2], document [−13.8,+1.3]), with NOINFO **60→38/70** (−31.4 pp; claim [−42.4,−21.1], document [−42.9,−20.3]). On those 70 reference-NOINFO pairs, 22 correct base NOINFO decisions become support/contradiction, none are corrected into NOINFO, and 23 semantic labels flip (the extra flip changes one wrong non-NOINFO label to another). Among **all complement labels**, 37 base-NOINFO predictions move to another label and one prediction moves into NOINFO; do not conflate this 37 with the 22 regressions on reference-NOINFO pairs. Positive-class gains (SUPPORT 65→74/78; CONTRADICT 4→7/11) partly cancel the losses; the 11 CONTRADICT pairs occupy only six claim clusters, so their interval [9.1,50.0] pp is particularly unstable. The complement base-to-reverse prediction marginal is S/C/N = 75/5/79 to 107/9/43. It had zero exact-repeat flips for Qwen.

The NOINFO shift is **not universal across systems**. In the complement, Llama 3.1 8B changes 33→15/70 NOINFO (−25.7 pp, claim [−36.8,−15.7], document [−36.4,−15.7]); Laya English 16→13/70, Laya Multilingual 8→8/70, and Jev 59→59/70. Laya English's title-removal correctness rises 171→203/339, but the frozen question/title mismatch forbids interpreting that as a pure causal title effect. The paper should foreground reference-class conditional sensitivity and system heterogeneity rather than promote a natural-mixture aggregate score alone.

## Identical-request cross-run audit

The old balanced 180 requests are byte-identical to their new-census counterparts in each of four conditions (720 request hashes independently checked). `overlap_audit.json` compares raw outputs case-by-case, listing every changed semantic prediction, gold label, old/fresh prediction, request hash, available LLM prompt-token hash, and batch ID/actual size. All **180/180 LLM prompt hashes match** per condition and local model. Both local runs used batch size 4, but the census's additional cases changed co-batch composition/order. The audit does **not** prove that batching caused the changes: CUDA numerical variation, near ties, run environment, and hosted service stochasticity remain alternatives.

For Qwen in particular, the original balanced run had base/reversal **104→101/180**, whereas the fresh census's same 180 cases yield **102→101/180**. Its base predictions differ on exactly two reference-NOINFO cases, `scifact3-dev:628:24512064` and `scifact3-dev:814:33387953`, each switching NOINFO→SUPPORT despite equal request and prompt-token hashes. `batch_context_audit.json` independently verifies different actual four-case co-batches for both, with close top-two code-logit margins (0–0.5); this is compatible with numerical decision instability but does not prove its cause. Two other reference-NOINFO cases change semantic label in reversal, but the reversal correct count remains 101. The NOINFO decline remains sizeable either way: original selected 54→30/60 (−40.0 pp, original paired CI [−53.3,−28.3]) versus fresh selected 52→30/60 (−36.7 pp, new claim/document CI [−48.3,−25.0]). These comparisons are a **run-stability sensitivity check**, not evidence for independent confirmation. Never pool old selected-180 predictions with new complement-159 outputs to claim a full-census result. Other models also have small identical-request changes (per-condition counts in `overlap_audit.json`); hosted Jev can differ across requests by design.

## Statistical and provenance limits

`summary.json` reports strict and valid-only accuracy, base/repeat/reverse/title correctness, robust correctness, corrections/regressions, paired semantic flips, excess flips over repeat, full 3×3+invalid confusion matrices, prediction marginals and base→reverse transition matrices for **all339**, **complement159**, and prior-selected180, each overall and by reference label. All raw-file counts/confusions/transitions were independently recomputed by a standard-library checker (`analysis_validation.json`). The bootstrap samples whole claims with all their pairs and conditions as the primary cluster; it repeats by whole document as sensitivity. Full-census NOINFO has 125 claim and 107 document clusters; complement NOINFO has 66 claim and 63 document clusters. The intervals are descriptive for a fixed dev source, not uncertainty over scientific domains, annotation quality, or a newly sampled population. The selected180 was balanced and claim/document disjoint; the full339 has natural cited-pair frequencies but can share claims/documents. None of these protocols adjudicate whether some official no-evidence entries are annotation omissions.

## Compact-paper insertion suggestion

“As a post-hoc sensitivity check, we reran all five systems on the complete 339 SciFact dev claim–cited-abstract pairs (138 SUPPORT/71 CONTRADICT/130 derived NOINFO) under the same four three-choice conditions, rather than splicing earlier predictions. In the 159 pairs absent from our balanced three-choice selection, Qwen's reversal reduces NOINFO correctness from 60 to 38 of 70 (paired claim-cluster 95% CI: −42.4 to −21.1 pp), while positive-class gains partly mask this as only 129→119/159 overall (CI: −13.7 to +1.2 pp); the full 339 shows 112→68/130 NOINFO and 231→220/339 overall. The complement is a same-source, post-hoc check—not an independent holdout—and 37 of its positives were previously tested under a different two-choice task; identical-request reruns show two Qwen balanced-base prediction changes, so all census numbers use the fresh complete run.”
