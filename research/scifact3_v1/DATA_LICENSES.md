# SciFact three-class attribution and redistribution boundary

This diagnostic is derived from **SciFact** by David Wadden, Shanchuan Lin, Kyle Lo, Lucy Lu Wang, Madeleine van Zuylen, Arman Cohan and Hannaneh Hajishirzi. Cite [*Fact or Fiction: Verifying Scientific Claims*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14974) and the [official dataset repository](https://github.com/allenai/scifact). The [upstream license notice](https://github.com/allenai/scifact/blob/master/LICENSE.md) assigns **CC BY 4.0** to `claims_*.jsonl` claims and evidence annotations, **ODC-By 1.0** to the S2ORC abstract corpus (`corpus.jsonl`), and Apache 2.0 to upstream code. Database attribution does not necessarily settle independent rights in every underlying paper abstract. This note is provenance information, not legal advice.

The local `frozen.json` contains selected original claims, paper titles and complete abstract sentences; it is Git-ignored rather than redistributed through this repository. Compared with the upstream files, we select 180 cited claim--document pairs with a fixed model-blind hash, join abstract sentences with newlines, derive `NOINFO` only for a listed cited document without an evidence entry, and produce three paired variants per base case. The `title_removed` variant omits only the paper title. Original source files are kept under ignored `data/external/domain_expansion_v1/`. The upstream archive and exact data-file SHA-256 hashes, and the reconstructed freeze hash, are in `manifest.json`.

If distributing the reconstructed freeze or abstracts separately, preserve attribution and upstream notices, describe the transformations above, and confirm any additional rights needed for that distribution. Neither SciFact's creators nor S2ORC endorse System1Bench.
